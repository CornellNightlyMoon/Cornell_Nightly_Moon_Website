About this site

Who writes it, how facts are labeled, why the accused are not named here, and how to reach the author.

Updated Oct 2, 2026, 2:30 pm Eastern

This site is written and maintained by one person, anonymously and independently. It is not affiliated with Cornell University, any party to the case, any law firm, or any news organization. It takes no position on anyone's guilt or liability. No one has been charged with a crime, and the allegations in a civil complaint are unproven until a court rules on them.

Every statement of fact is labeled by what supports it, and every quotation is attributed to the document or report it came from. Where this site says "what it can mean," it is describing possibilities under New York law as the statutes and cases are written, not predicting an outcome.

On names

The seven individual defendants are named in the public complaint and in major news coverage. This site describes them by the complaint's numbering rather than by name because none has been charged with a crime, two say Cornell found them not responsible, and the allegations against each differ substantially. Readers who want the names can read the complaint, linked on the Documents page. Public officials and attorneys who have spoken publicly are named.

Support resources

  • Advocacy Center of Tompkins County, 24-hour hotline: 607-277-5000. Confidential, off campus.
  • Cornell SHARE (Sexual Harassment and Assault Response and Education): share.cornell.edu. Lists confidential and non-confidential options, including Cornell Health's Victim Advocacy Program.
  • RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline: 1-800-656-4673 or hotline.rainn.org. Confidential, 24 hours.
  • Reporting. Cornell University Police: 607-255-1111. Ithaca Police Department: 607-272-3245, or 911 in an emergency. Cornell's Title IX office accepts reports regardless of whether a police report is made. Under Education Law § 6442, a student who reports a sexual assault in good faith is not subject to campus discipline for alcohol or drug use at the time.
